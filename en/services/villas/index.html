Residential precast

Villas

Elegant, durable precast structures for villa projects and residential communities.

Precast concrete gives villa developments a consistent architectural finish and a construction programme that runs independently of the structural frame. Elements are cast to the project's design, then delivered and installed on site.

Mouldex reference works include precast triangle elements for Bulgari Ocean Villas and GRC panels for the Lamborghini Villas project, alongside precast boundary walls for Elysian Mansions at Tilal Al Ghaf.

What this covers

  • Villa facade panels
  • Feature and triangle elements
  • GRC panel installation
  • Residential community works

Where it is applied

  • Villa communities
  • Waterfront villas
  • Gated residential developments
