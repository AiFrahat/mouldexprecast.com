Precast Joints Sealant Works

Joint sealing that protects precast structures and keeps facades weathertight.

Precast elements meet at joints, and those joints are what keeps a facade weathertight. Sealant works cover joint preparation, the correct sealant system for the movement expected, and a clean, consistent finish that protects the structure over its service life.

Sealant works are delivered as part of the company's precast installation scope, alongside the cladding, wall and structural packages.

What this covers

  • Joint preparation
  • Sealant application
  • Movement joint treatment
  • Weatherproofing

Where it is applied

  • Facade panels
  • Boundary walls
  • Structural precast elements
