About Mouldex

A precast provider for Dubai and the GCC

A Dubai-based precast contractor specialising in precast erection contracting, with an industrial licence for prefabricated houses contracting and technical services.

Company overview

Precast, from pre-qualification to installation

Mouldex Precast Contracting L.L.C stands as a provider of comprehensive precast solutions in Dubai, UAE. With a commitment to quality, the company specialises in precast erection contracting, holding an industrial licence for prefabricated houses contracting and technical services.

Led by the seasoned expertise of General Manager Samy Abdel Aziz, who brings over 20 years of experience in the precast industry, the company has emerged as a reliable and innovative force in the GCC region.

The company operates as a private company with an industrial licence for prefabricated houses contracting and technical services works, and its commitment to quality and innovation sets it apart in the market.

Turnkey precast solutions
